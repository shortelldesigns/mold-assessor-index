About Mold Assessor Index

Mold Assessor Index is an independent public directory compiled by Shortell Designs. It lists people and companies who already appear on official Florida, Texas, and New York mold license extracts. Louisiana is a verify link because the state does not publish a comparable free file. It is a reading of those records, not a new credential.

This index lists Florida licensed mold assessors, Texas mold assessment companies, and New York licensed mold contractors. Louisiana: verify on LSLBC.

What this is not

  • Not a laboratory and not a mold assessment or remediation company.
  • Published by Shortell Designs as an independent directory — not as a laboratory or contractor service.
  • Not endorsed by the U.S. EPA, Florida DBPR, Texas TDLR, New York DOL, or Louisiana LSLBC.
  • Not a ranking, marketplace, or referral desk. There is no paid placement on professional lists.
  • Not Exclusive Live Calls. There is no live-call page, no tracking numbers, and no click-to-call.
  • Not a national mold directory. Most states do not license this work. IICRC and AIHA directories were not scraped (private terms).

Where the names come from

Retrieved 2026-09-01.

  • Florida: 6,353 MRSA/MRSR rows (2,991 current/active assessors, 2,882 current/active remediators, 480 current/inactive) from DBPR lic07mold.csv, last modified 2026-08-29. 6,721 rows in the file; CE providers and courses omitted.
  • Texas: 204 current assessment companies and 465 current remediation companies from TDLR vsMoldAssessmentCompany.csv and vsMoldRemediationCompany.csv, last modified 2026-09-01. Individual TDLR extracts were not transcribed.
  • New York: 794 active assessment contractors and 934 active remediation contractors from Mold Contractor Licenses (Four Year Window), last modified 2026-09-01. Individual licenses were not in this file.
  • Louisiana: not transcribed. See verify on LSLBC.

No licensee, phone number, or license number was invented. Phone numbers from the official lists are republished as text only.
